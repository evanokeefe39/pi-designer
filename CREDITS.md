# Credits

The pi-designer **knowledge layer** (`knowledge/`) is curated from
educational design and UI/UX content posted by these creators on Instagram.
Their publicly shared tips, workflows, tool recommendations, and aesthetic
breakdowns were analysed and manually curated into the reference docs.

Each creator's content contributed concepts, resources, workflows, or tips
that appear across `knowledge/resources.md`, `knowledge/aesthetics.md`,
`knowledge/principles.md`, and `knowledge/workflows.md`.

## About the curation

~28 educational Instagram posts were analysed via Gemini, then manually
curated into the four `knowledge/` docs (resources, aesthetics, principles,
workflows). The analysis extracted concepts, workflows, tool
recommendations, and design tips — no original media, no personal data,
and no gated content triggers are included in pi-designer.

The knowledge layer is static markdown; it does not reference the scraping
pipeline by path. For the ~493 posts in the saved list not yet analysed,
re-curation is manual and ad hoc.
